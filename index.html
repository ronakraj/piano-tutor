Connecting your Roland over Bluetooth
  • iPad/iPhone: Safari and Chrome on iOS can't do Web MIDI. Install the free Web MIDI Browser app (by Takashi Mizuhiki), open its settings (gear icon) to connect your Roland's Bluetooth MIDI, then enter this page's address (swipe down from the top to show the address bar) and tap “Connect piano”. Landscape works best.
  • Otherwise use Chrome or Edge (laptop) or Chrome on Android.
  • Mac: put the piano in Bluetooth pairing mode, then Audio MIDI Setup → Window → Show MIDI Studio → Bluetooth icon → Connect. Then press "Connect piano" here.
  • Windows: pair it in Settings → Bluetooth. Support for Bluetooth MIDI in Chrome on Windows can be hit-and-miss; a USB cable is the reliable fallback.
  • No piano handy? Click the on-screen keys, or use the computer keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K (C4–C5; Z/X shift octave).
  • Scores: export MusicXML (.musicxml / .mxl) from MuseScore or similar. For a photo/PDF/screenshot, send it to Claude to transcribe into MusicXML.
